- chinese pickup
    - wires (tie red and white together)
        - red: north coil start
        - green: north coil end
        - white: south coil start
        - black: south coil end
- adjustment
    - adjusting truss rod
        - capo 1st fret and 14th fret
        - look at the gap on the 7th fret, should be 0.3mm
        - measure when tuned; adjust 45° at a time and wait 6hrs between adjustments
    - adjust action
        - capo 1st fret
        - measure at 14th fret
            - 2.5mm for the thickest string and 1.5mm for the thinnest should be fine
    - check nut height
        - remove capo at 1st fret
        - gap on 1st fret should be close to the gap that was at the 2nd fret with the capo
    - adjust intonation
        - check notes are the same on the 0th and 12th frets
        - if not, adjust with saddle position
